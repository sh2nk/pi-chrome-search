# P6 — M6: `web_research` + codemode comparison

Date: 2026-11-06
Owner: orchestrator
Verdict: **PASS**

## V6.1 — budgets are exact

`node --test tests/unit/web_research.test.ts` (24 tests):

- quick 1 search + 2 reads, normal 2 + 4, deep 4 + 8 — exactly the allowance, never more (spies on the
  injected `search`/`read` seams), per-call reset verified;
- early stop when coverage is satisfied (normal finished at 1 search / 2 reads) and when the output cap is
  reached (deep cut to 3 reads with `truncated:true`);
- the cap matrix `[400, 600, 800, 1200, 2500] × 3 budgets` always renders `text.length ≤ cap`;
- abort mid-search and mid-read returns `E_CANCELLED` and the read seam is not called after the abort;
- the tool never acquires a page itself (`acquiredPages === 0`), so sibling tools own tab lifetime.

## V6.2 — evidence integrity

Every claim carries a `sourceUrl` **and** citations, and every cited URL is one the SERP actually returned;
domains equal `domainOf(url)`; duplicate URLs (tracking params) collapse; at most one refinement per coverage
round, and the refinement must add a new term and a new domain or the loop stops. No fabricated domains.

## V6.3 — works with the reflex off

Disabled + `localOnly:true` → zero triage/coverage calls and the fixed budget runs to completion. Enabled →
the injected reflex order is honoured; a throwing reflex falls back and still respects 2+4.

## V6.4 — three paths, one corpus (offline)

`node bench/codemode.ts` (deterministic, no model, no network, no Chrome; `--self-test` 11/11):

| path | chars | tokens | agent calls | nested calls | recall@5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| direct (`web_search` n=5 + 2 reads @6000) | 391 660 | 97 926 | 90 | 0 | 0.789 |
| codemode (one script, filtered JSON, 600-char read heads) | 98 971 | 24 752 | 30 | 90 | 0.789 |
| `web_research` normal | 60 793 | 15 211 | 30 | 174 | 0.644 |

By budget: quick 54 126 chars / recall 0.389, normal 60 793 / 0.644, deep 43 227 / 0.656.

Honesty notes recorded in `bench/RESULTS-CODEMODE.md`: codemode's −74.7% is the blind truncation of two page
bodies to 600 chars (at 6000 chars the same path costs **+8.4%**), the script text is charged per query, the
read bodies are proxied from the three committed read fixtures (cost shape, not that URL's content), and
model/agent thinking time is not measured. Recommendation: direct by default; codemode when a large
intermediate can be filtered client-side; `web_research` when citations matter more than recall@5.

## Known follow-up

`web_research` sizes `excerptChars` from the read allowance, so an early-stopping `deep` run can return
shorter excerpts than `normal` (43 227 vs 60 793 chars for the same evidence). A tool-side sizing choice, not
a gate failure; recorded for the next iteration.
