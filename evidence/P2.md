Policy D13: reserve the results floor (n items, snippets 100) before capping the AI; sources trimmed first, then
prose, whole AI block only below 6 chars. URLs are never cut. `vw` verified by the floor-removal replay
(6 tests fail without the reservation; the V3.5 fixture regresses to 2 items).

## Deviations

- **Prompt surface:** descriptions are 248/900 (V2.6), but `description + JSON schema` is 248 + 777 = **1025**
  chars (`engines` union alone 337). SPEC §8's literal "descriptions + schemas ≤900" is therefore exceeded
  by 125 chars with one tool. V2.6 gates descriptions only; P4-D measures the combined surface and may trim
  the unions or amend the wording. Recorded, not silently waived.
- **DDG pagination deferred:** `page>1` on DDG is served as page 1 and reported as `pageIgnored:true`
  (SPEC §6.3 mentions `&s=<offset>`; frozen `Query.page` is google/yandex-only). See PLAN §5.1.
- **JSON fitting order:** when the JSON view exceeds the cap, the AI is trimmed first (D13), then the snippet
  ladder 100/60/30/0, then tail results; below the `JSON_MIN_ENVELOPE` (85 chars) the minimal envelope is
  returned and `truncated:true` (a rendering cap is not a caller error).
- **Humanize stale marker:** a marker already present on the engine home page resolves immediately; adapters
  must pass `selector`+`minCount` to avoid extracting the home page. Documented in `humanize.ts`.
- **Artifacts pulled forward** from P4-C so V3.6 has snapshots; 25 tests including a token-carrier matrix
  (`data-ved`, `jsdata`, `style`, inline JSON, cookie forms) and numeric same-second prune ordering.
