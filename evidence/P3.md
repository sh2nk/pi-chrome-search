## Harness

`tests/helpers/dom.ts` runs an adapter's extraction function twice from one source: live via
`page.evaluate("(" + extractFromDocument + ")(document, …)")`, offline via `node:vm` over a `linkedom`
parse of the fixture. `tests/unit/dom_harness.test.ts` proves the harness itself is real (parses the
largest Google capture, finds organics, fails on a closure). `linkedom` is a devDependency only.

## Deviations

- **`/goto` resolution lives in the tool layer** (`src/extract/goto.ts` + `web_search`), not in the Google
  adapter: adapters stay pure DOM extractors. Unresolvable wrappers are kept; success is cached 24 h.
- **`ToolDeps.fetchImpl`** (optional, `contract:` commit) makes the resolver injectable so offline tests
  cannot reach Google.
- **CDP broker** `research/probes/07-cdp-broker.mjs`: Chrome 136+ has no permanent Allow; the broker holds
  one connection and serialises short-lived clients, so a work session costs one click instead of one per
  capture. Live work stays orchestrator-only and serial.
